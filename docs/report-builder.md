# Reports builder

Choose a category and dataset, or open **Start from a template** or **Saved reports** in the header. Choose a display structure, select fields and conditions, preview the results and choose **Save & export**. The field library groups standard, calculated, custom and manual columns and supports search. The final step can export the preview to Excel and save or update the configuration. The preview uses the original Studio table and its column, sort, filter and drilldown controls.

The Reports stepper follows the Email Updates motion and glass palette. The selected pill uses a layout spring, a brief color bloom and pointer-driven dye that slows and fades after exit. Reduced motion keeps the static glass appearance. Report steps become selectable as you reach them.

## Dataset and metric contract

| Dataset | Row and relationship | Scope, date and measures |
|---|---|---|
| Client directory | One Client, keyed by business client ID | Client-wide profile, registration record and safe custom fields. The added date comes from client creation. A recorded registration number does not certify legal registration status. |
| Client and FY overview | One client evaluated for a selected FY | Summaries join by client ID and FY before any cross-domain columns are combined. Financial Year Records requires an FY record. Contact counts use ClientContact and Person. Document counts in legacy client summaries are client-wide. |
| PIBO targets and PWP credits | One client and selected FY | PIBO coverage combines FinancialYear targets with incoming CreditTransaction quantities. Cap by client, category and Recycling/EOL type. Coverage uses verified target as the denominator. Raw recorded credits remain separately visible. PWP availability is generated less outbound credits. |
| Annual Returns and invoice tracking | One client and selected FY | AnnualReturn status and workflow checkpoints. Sale and purchase coverage uses the most recently edited invoice record for each direction and month. A recorded nil month counts toward coverage. Missing directional evidence stays unknown. |
| Invoice months and CPCB uploads | One client/direction/month or one UploadRecord | FY coverage uses the coverage month. Uploads use record creation date and report recorded direction, invoice count and category quantities. Upload quantities have no reliable credit-to-upload linkage, so achieved-but-not-uploaded is unavailable. |
| Quotations and revisions | One Quotation or one QuotationRevision | FY comes from the quotation. Dates are quotation creation and revision creation, respectively. The quotation register counts only current-revision value. Revision-history values are non-additive. Accepted quotations await billing until a bill cites their ID. Unlinked client records remain visible. |
| Bills and balances | One Billing record | Bill date and assigned FY. Allocated payments are current settlements for each bill, including applied advances. Outstanding is computed per bill before aggregation. A client overpayment cannot settle a different bill. |
| Payment records and reconciliation | One Payment record | Assigned FY and payment date. Explicit allocations require an existing bill belonging to the same client and support later-year receipts. A single eligible Annual Return bill in the assigned FY permits the legacy fallback. Broken or ambiguous links show a review reason. Current advance balances and applications are separate from cash receipts. Historical as-of advance balances are unavailable. |
| Credit movements | One CreditTransaction | Assigned FY and transaction date. Seller and buyer remain separate columns so the transaction amount is counted once. Missing rates remain blank. |
| Tracker, document and work registers | One ClientTrackerEntry, Document or ClientWorkItem | Entries use parent tracker FY and entry update time. Documents and work items include unassigned records, with upload date or due date as appropriate. Documents expose inventory metadata. |
| Campaigns and email activity | One TrackerEmailCampaign or EmailLog | Administrator-only metadata. Campaign scope comes from the saved FY snapshot. Email log scope can be unassigned. Dates come from campaign creation or the recorded send/draft date. Bodies, recipients, message IDs and credentials are excluded. |

The registry and field definitions specify permitted measures, grouping and comparison types. Numeric aggregates use recorded values, with blanks when there are no known values. Date-only upper bounds include the complete UTC calendar day. Grouped drilldowns use exact set membership for list-valued dimensions. Preview totals cover all matching rows, and Excel reruns the same applied configuration without display pagination. The workbook and preview can differ if source records change between requests. Ready-made Excel workbooks and custom client exports retain their own scope and formats.

## Advanced billing analysis

Choose **Finance → Billing** and select Client-wise, Bill-wise or Charge-wise in **Display structure**. Client-wise rows represent a company and financial year. Add comparison years in **Fields & conditions**, then group by financial year or use the existing two-dimension pivot. The older billing summary and bill-register configurations keep their dataset identifiers. The legacy bill register uses assigned-year allocation rules. Use **Billing** or **Billing & payments** to include valid explicit links whose stored payment FY differs from the bill FY.

Bill and charge scope is evaluated on each charge and its parent bill. For example, General bill type plus EPR Targets classification selects only annotated EPR charges on General bills. Use **Charge types on bill** or **Charge classifications on bill** with AND conditions to require multiple values on the same bill. Add a **Charge type** or **Charge classification** condition to select the charges to total. Result filters run after the chosen reporting level is calculated. Company and bill drilldowns carry the original bill and charge scope into the next level.

Company and bill rows show selected charge gross alongside full bill gross, allocated payments and outstanding. Each bill contributes once to full-bill amounts. Charge rows expose the recorded tax split and rate where known. Payment records settle bills, so component balances are unavailable. Advance-only clients appear in the Payment register. Mixed or missing rates stay blank. The report splits a fixed EPR charge into target lines only when the stored gross and tax amounts reconcile. Legacy target gross remains available with unknown category detail.

Government and Consultancy are stored charge types. Registration / Migration, Annual Return, General Billing / Consultancy and the other classifications come from explicit annotations and existing defaults. Use either dimension independently. Company and bill views offer conditional columns for charge types, classifications and category and credit-type combinations.

Billing queries project candidate records and push down required FY, bill type, bill ID and supported date conditions. Payments use explicit bill links and the existing single-candidate legacy rule. Candidate siblings are checked before allocation. Computed filters and report totals are evaluated across scoped candidates before server pagination. Report reads use existing indexes and apply safety limits.

## Storage and query boundaries

Use **Dataset selection** for the report area and source. **Display structure** sets the layout, month basis where available, and up to three grouping levels. For Billing, expand **Bill and charge scope** in that step to select the financial year, bill type and charge conditions. **Fields & conditions** sets columns, measures and filters on the report rows. The preview includes group subtotals. Excel includes all subtotals on separate sheets. Pivot uses two grouping dimensions.

**Billing & payments** combines bill rows, recorded direct receipts and current advance balances. Use **Client totals** or **Month-wise** in Display structure. Bill gross, allocated payments and outstanding use the bill FY and the current allocations on the bill. A valid explicit link to the same client's bill can settle a bill from a prior FY. Direct cash uses the payment date and excludes advance applications. Monthly outstanding is the current balance on bills issued in that month. Available advances are grouped by their stored FY. The application can reduce or remove advance receipt records, so those records cannot establish complete historical advance receipts. Each stored payment row supports one bill link.

**Payments** can use payment-date or stored financial-year scope. Existing saved payment reports default to the stored FY. **Advances** lists current balances and applications. Reports recognize applications recorded with the source field, the legacy “Advance Applied” payment mode, or the legacy “applied from advance” note. Unresolved or ambiguous allocations appear in the reconciliation columns.

**Received invoices** offers client coverage, April–March columns and individual monthly status rows. NIL contributes to months covered. Months received count physical invoice receipt status only. Partial, missing and unknown counts are displayed separately. The stored status field is available for older saved reports.

**EPR Targets** can be reported by category and obligation type. Coverage totals use verified covered quantity divided by verified target obligation for the selected rows. **Quotations** offers entry, revision and current-revision charge layouts. **Credit transactions** offers a transaction view and client purchase and sale rows with separate directional measures. Category values require reconciliation to the transaction gross.

- Saved reports belong to the signed-in account and contain configuration only. Older configs remain readable. New configs use version 2 and may include typed AND/OR rules with three-valued evaluation.
- Administrators can create and update manual fields. The unique value key is field ID, client ID and scope FY. An empty scope FY means client-wide. Values persist separately from saved report definitions. The directory exposes client-wide fields, while client/FY datasets can also expose FY-specific fields.
- Data selection controls which client summary collections are read. Client search narrows linked lookups and a simple category filter can restrict the client query. Detail loaders project reportable fields, apply required stored date bounds where supported and bound most source reads. The result page is sliced on the server after filters, metrics and grouping, which still requires evaluating the relevant candidates. Broad detail reports stop at a safety limit and can require a narrower scope.
- Reports read operational data. Manual values and saved reports write to their own stores. The dedicated legacy Billing views retain explicit billing-category annotations.
